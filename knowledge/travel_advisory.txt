TOPIC: General International & Domestic Travel Advisories and Visa Disclaimer

1. Official Visa Disclaimer & Legal Notice:
Visa rules, transit regulations, border entry criteria, and fee structures change frequently without prior public notice. 
Information provided by this automated trip planner is for preliminary guidance and itinerary planning only. 
NEVER rely solely on automated summaries for legal or border-crossing authorization.
All travelers must independently verify their eligibility directly with the official embassy, high commission, or immigration authority of their destination country prior to departing or purchasing non-refundable tickets.

2. Passport Validity & Condition:
For virtually all international travel, your passport must have at least 6 months validity remaining beyond your intended return date, and at least two blank immigration pages. Damaged or frayed passports may be refused by airlines at check-in or by immigration authorities on arrival.

3. Travel Insurance & Health:
Comprehensive travel insurance covering emergency medical evacuation, trip cancellation, and baggage loss is strongly recommended for all international trips. Verify if any vaccinations (such as Yellow Fever for specific transit zones or routine immunizations) are required before travel.

4. Domestic Travel (India / Munnar, Kerala):
For domestic travel within India (e.g., visiting Munnar, Kerala):
- Indian citizens require a valid government photo ID (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License) for hotel check-ins and airport security.
- Foreign nationals must carry valid Indian visa/e-Visa and passport.
- Protected environmental zones such as Eravikulam National Park (Rajamalai) require prior entry ticket booking, and specific trekking areas may require Kerala Forest Department permits.
- Monsoon season (June to August) can cause localized landslides along Ghat roads in Idukki district; caution and local weather monitoring are advised.

5. Emergency Preparedness:
Keep digital and physical copies of passport bio pages, travel insurance, hotel confirmations, and emergency embassy contact numbers stored separately from your original documents.
