COUNTRY: Malaysia
TOPIC: Visa-Free Entry, MDAC Arrival Card, and Immigration Protocols

1. Visa Policy for Indian Citizens:
Under Malaysia's Tourism Visa Liberalization scheme, Indian citizens enjoy visa-free entry to Malaysia for social and tourism visits of up to 30 days per entry. This visa-free facility does not require prior visa application, provided all general entry conditions are met. Stays cannot be extended beyond the 30-day limit under the visa-free scheme.

2. Mandatory Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC):
All international foreign travelers must complete and submit the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC) online within 3 days prior to arrival in Malaysia.
Submission is free through the official Immigration Department of Malaysia portal (imigresen-online.imi.gov.my). Failure to submit MDAC prior to arrival may cause processing delays or denial of entry at immigration checkpoints.

3. General Entry Requirements:
- Valid passport with at least 6 months validity from the date of arrival in Malaysia.
- Confirmed round-trip flight booking / return ticket to home country or onward flight ticket.
- Verifiable proof of hotel accommodation or address in Malaysia.
- Proof of sufficient financial funds (credit card, bank statement, or cash) to sustain the stay.

4. Customs & Prohibited Goods:
Strict drug laws apply in Malaysia with heavy statutory penalties. Controlled imports and taxable items must be declared at red channel customs on arrival.
