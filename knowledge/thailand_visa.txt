COUNTRY: Thailand
TOPIC: Visa Requirements, Visa Exemption, and Tourism Entry Guidelines

1. Visa Policy for Indian Citizens:
The Government of Thailand has granted visa exemption for Indian passport holders for tourism and short-term business purposes, permitting stays of up to 60 days per entry (extendable once for an additional 30 days at an immigration office in Thailand).
Alternatively, when visa exemption schemes are not applicable, Indian citizens are eligible for Visa on Arrival (VoA) for stays up to 15 days or can apply for an e-Visa through the official Thai e-Visa portal (thaievisa.go.th) before departure.

2. Mandatory Documents for Entry:
- Passport with minimum 6 months validity from the planned date of departure from Thailand.
- Confirmed return or onward travel ticket within the allowed duration of stay.
- Verifiable proof of hotel booking or residential accommodation address in Thailand.
- Proof of adequate finances: minimum 10,000 THB per person (or 20,000 THB per family) in cash or equivalent foreign currency.

3. Immigration Clearance:
Immigration officers may randomly verify financial means, accommodation vouchers, and return tickets at the port of entry (Suvarnabhumi BKK, Don Mueang DMK, Phuket HKT). Travelers entering under visa exemption must not work or engage in unauthorized employment.

4. Customs & Prohibited Items:
E-cigarettes, vaping equipment, and shisha are completely illegal in Thailand. Possessing or importing them can result in hefty fines, confiscation, and detention. Severe penalties apply for controlled substances.

SOURCE DISCLAIMER:
Information synthesized from the Royal Thai Embassy and Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) immigration guidance. Visa exemption schemes and entry regulations can be modified or revoked by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs at any time. Travelers must check the official Royal Thai Embassy or official immigration portal prior to departure.
