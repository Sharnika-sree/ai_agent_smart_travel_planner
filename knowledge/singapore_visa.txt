COUNTRY: Singapore
TOPIC: Entry Requirements, Visa Regulations, and SG Arrival Card

1. Visa Policy for Indian Citizens:
Indian passport holders require a valid visa to enter Singapore. There is NO visa-on-arrival facility for standard Indian passport holders arriving for tourism or business. 
Travelers must apply for an entry visa before departure through an authorized visa agent, strategic partner, or a local Singapore sponsor via Singapore Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) SAVE system.
Visa processing typically takes 3 to 5 working days (excluding day of submission, weekends, and public holidays).

2. Mandatory Electronic SG Arrival Card (SGAC):
All international travelers (including tourists and Singapore citizens) must submit an SG Arrival Card with Electronic Health Declaration online within 3 days prior to arrival in Singapore. The SG Arrival Card is completely FREE to submit on the official ICA website (eservices.ica.gov.sg/sgarrivalcard). Beware of commercial third-party scam sites charging fees.

3. General Entry Requirements:
- A passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the date of departure from Singapore.
- Confirmed return or onward flight tickets.
- Sufficient financial funds for the duration of the stay in Singapore.
- Proof of accommodation or hotel booking reservation.
- Visa for onward destination (if applicable).

4. Transit Visa (Visa-Free Transit Facility - VFTF):
Indian nationals holding valid long-term visas or residence permits issued by Australia, Canada, Germany, Japan, New Zealand, Switzerland, United Kingdom, or United States may be eligible for the 96-hour Visa-Free Transit Facility (VFTF) when transiting through Singapore on direct onward journeys. Eligibility criteria are strictly assessed by ICA officers at entry.

5. Customs and Prohibitions:
Strict customs regulations apply: chewing gum, e-cigarettes / vaping devices, and contraband items are strictly banned. Severe penalties apply for drug trafficking.
